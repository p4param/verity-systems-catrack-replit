
# PX-003 — Workspace Design Principles

**Document ID:** PX-003
**Title:** Workspace Design Principles
**Status:** Approved
**Version:** 1.0
**Category:** Product Library → Experience Framework

---

# Purpose

Define the standard structure and behavior of all workspaces within Catrack.

---

# Workspace Philosophy

A workspace is the operational environment surrounding a business object.

It combines information, actions, collaboration, and progress into a single contextual experience.

---

# Standard Workspace Anatomy

Every workspace should be composed of the following sections where applicable:

1. Workspace Header
2. Operational Health
3. Timeline
4. Action Center
5. KPI Summary
6. Operational Panels
7. Tasks
8. Activity Feed
9. Documents
10. Communications
11. Analytics

---

# Workspace Characteristics

Every workspace should be:

* Context aware
* Role aware
* Workflow aware
* Metadata driven
* Responsive
* Extensible
* Consistent across the product

---

# Operational Panels

Panels should present live operational information rather than static forms.

Typical panels include:

* Planning
* Kitchen
* Procurement
* Inventory
* Staff
* Equipment
* Vehicles
* Finance
* Risks
* Documents
* Notes

Panels should be independently refreshable and expandable.

---

# Action Center

Every workspace contains an Action Center responsible for presenting:

* Recommended actions
* Pending approvals
* Blockers
* Deadlines
* Critical notifications

The Action Center should proactively guide the user toward successful completion of business activities.

---

# Timeline

Every workspace maintains a business timeline that records meaningful operational events rather than technical audit details.

---

# Workspace Evolution

Workspaces should evolve naturally as business processes progress.

Different lifecycle stages should reveal different information and recommended actions.
