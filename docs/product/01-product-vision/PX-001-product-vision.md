# Purpose

Define the long-term vision, mission, and product identity of Catrack.

This document serves as the highest-level product reference for all future business, UX, and functional design decisions.

---

# Product Vision

> **Catrack is the Operating System for Catering Businesses.**

Catrack is not simply an ERP that stores business records.

It is a modern operational platform that helps catering organizations plan, coordinate, execute, and analyze every event with confidence.

The system should become the central workspace through which the entire business operates.

---

# Mission

Enable catering businesses to deliver exceptional events through intelligent workflows, contextual guidance, operational visibility, and elegant user experiences.

---

# Product Goals

The product should:

* Reduce operational complexity.
* Reduce training requirements.
* Reduce manual coordination.
* Increase operational visibility.
* Improve decision making.
* Prevent operational mistakes.
* Encourage standardized business processes.
* Scale from small catering companies to enterprise organizations.

---

# Core Principles

* Workspace First
* Guide, Don't Train
* Health Before Data
* Reduce Cognitive Load
* Progressive Disclosure
* Contextual Actions
* Business Language over Technical Language
* Minimal Navigation
* One Primary Purpose per Screen

---

# User Promise

Whenever a user opens Catrack they should immediately understand:

* Where they are.
* What is happening.
* What requires attention.
* What should happen next.
* Whether operations are healthy.

The software should continuously guide users instead of expecting them to remember procedures.

---

# Success Criteria

Catrack succeeds when users describe it as:

* Easy to understand.
* Easy to operate.
* Pleasant to use.
* Operationally intelligent.
* Different from traditional ERP systems.

---

# Guiding Statement

> **We are not building another ERP. We are redefining what an ERP feels like.**
